What is Shared Planet?

Shared Planet™ is the first color cosmetics brand to put the pursuit of social and environmental change before all else. The brand, and its products, bring visibility to endangered animals and donates 10% of proceeds to animal-related charities across the world. Shared Planet™ has built its foundation on maintaining a vegan and cruelty-free status, and they partner with social media influencers, who are equally passionate about animals, who use their platforms to help spread awareness.
