What is PUR Spices?

PUR Spices is a line of global sauces and spice blends that are free from the top 9 allergens, without sacrificing flavor. Founded by a mom on a mission to make cooking more accessible and safe for her food-allergy family, PUR Spices has carved out a niche in the clean-label aisle.

How Did We Help?

Objective

To develop an organic social strategy and email marketing campaign for globally-inspired sauce and spice brand, PUR Spices LTD and to leverage key messaging during promotional periods and evergreen moments.

Marketing Strategy

Developed a consistent social cadence @purspices, focused on product versatility and clean ingredients. Then carried over core messaging to speak to both PUR Spice's pre-existing consumer base and excite a new audience.
